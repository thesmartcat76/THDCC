About the Twisted Hot Dawg Coding Club

The Twisted Hot Dawg Coding Club (THDCC) is a community of students who are passionate about coding and technology. We meet regularly to work on projects, share knowledge, and support each other in our coding journeys. Our club is open to students of all skill levels, from beginners to experienced programmers. We believe in fostering a collaborative and inclusive environment where everyone can learn and grow together.
